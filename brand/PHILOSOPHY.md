# Suveda — Logo & Brand Mark

## The idea: *one letter, one journey*

The mark is the letter **S** — for **Suveda** — drawn as a single, unbroken
**path**. It is not just an initial; it is the shape of a journey: it begins
high on the right, sweeps across, and lands low on the left. A departure and
an arrival, held in one continuous gesture.

Suveda is an app about a move — packing, papers, budgets, goodbyes — but
underneath the checklists it is a love letter: *"whether far or near, I am
close to your heart."* The logo had to carry both the **practical** (a place,
a route, a destination) and the **tender** (warmth, a guiding light, the
feeling of being carried).

## How the meaning is built in

- **The S as a route.** The two open terminals are a "from" and a "to." The
  curve is the distance between two homes — and the fact that it's a single
  stroke says the journey is one connected thing, not a break.
- **The sunrise gradient.** The stroke warms as it descends — gold at the
  start (sunrise, a wish, beginnings) into terracotta (earth, arrival,
  home). New day, new ground.
- **The teal ground.** The deep teal tile is the Gulf and the calm of the
  destination — Abu Dhabi waiting at the other end.
- **The star.** A small gold sparkle sits at the journey's beginning — the
  guiding star, the *"always with you,"* the bit of magic that makes a move
  feel like an adventure instead of a loss.

## Craft principles

- **Legible at 16px and at 1024px.** The glyph is bold, geometric, and built
  from clean circular arcs, so it holds its shape as a favicon and as a
  home-screen icon alike.
- **One mark, two grounds.** A warm-on-cool *bold* version for app icons and
  a *light* version for favicons and on-paper use — same silhouette, so the
  brand stays recognizable everywhere.
- **The existing palette, disciplined.** Teal `#2A6E6B`, terracotta
  `#C4714A`, gold `#D4A853`, cream `#FAF7F2` — the colors already in the
  app, now resolved into a single confident gesture instead of many small
  elements.
